# Status reports

Status reports helps Slice project managers turn their consultants' updates into a draft client status report, using their own AI assistant. It gives you one place with the report template, a worked example and a prompt you can copy. You review the draft, paste it into the Slides template and send the PDF yourself. The app stores nothing and sends nothing. It is still a work in progress and its wording may change.

## Before you start: sensitive and NDA projects

**If your project is under an NDA, or is on the sensitive-project list, do not use the prompt.** Never paste that project's material into an AI assistant. Compile the report by hand or in a tool Slice has approved for it. The VP of Operations keeps the sensitive-project list; ask them if you are unsure.

For every other project: anything you paste into your AI assistant leaves Slice for that assistant's provider. Paste only what the report needs, and nothing about other clients or members.

## How to write a report in four steps

1. **Gather your notes.** Collect each consultant's completed work since the last report, their plan for the next period, any blockers, and upcoming dates (meetings, deliverables, client events). Ask each consultant for a few lines in this shape: workstream, completed, planned, blockers, and any dates.
2. **Copy the prompt.** Open **Status reports** from the App Launcher and choose **Copy prompt**, or copy it from the end of this guide. Replace the words in square brackets, say whether the project is under an NDA, and paste your notes at the end. Send it to your AI assistant.
3. **Review the draft.** Check it against the drafting rules below. Answer everything the assistant lists under "Questions for the PM". You are responsible for every word the client sees.
4. **Paste into the Slides template and send it as a PDF.** Save it as `MM.DD.YY Status Report #N - Client`, for example `10.20.26 Status Report #1 - Harbor Light`. Email it to the client with the VP of Operations and the VP of Client Relations on CC. Ask the VP of Operations for the current template if you do not have it.

## Report format

1. **Title slide:** "Status Report #N", the date, the consultants with their initials, and the project manager.
2. **Completed and planned work:** one or more slides. Each workstream is headed "Workstream #N - Name (consultants)" and has two columns, Completed and Planned, each labelled with its own date range. Every bullet starts with the consultant's initials in brackets, such as [AB]. A slide holds up to two workstreams.
3. **Upcoming schedule:** a table with the columns Event Title, Event Date(s) and Notes.
4. **File:** named `MM.DD.YY Status Report #N - Client` and sent as a PDF.

## Drafting rules

- Use only what the notes say. Never invent results, dates or numbers; list anything missing under "Questions for the PM".
- Past tense in Completed, future tense in Planned. One line per bullet, led by the consultant's initials.
- Group by workstream, then by consultant. Use professional, client-ready wording.
- Put blockers in Planned or in your cover note, not hidden in Completed.
- End with a short checklist: gaps, dates to confirm, anything that looks confidential.

Two fixes for the current template: label the Planned column with the **next** period's dates (the template repeats the Completed dates there), and sort each bullet by tense, so finished work goes in Completed even if a consultant wrote it under Planned.

## The example

The app shows a complete example report for Harbor Light Community Fund, a made-up client, with invented consultants and dates. Use it to see how a finished report reads.

## The prompt

Replace the words in square brackets before you send it.

```text
You are helping a Slice Consulting project manager draft a client status report.
Use ONLY the notes I paste below. Do not invent results, dates or numbers.
If something is missing, list it under "Questions for the PM".

Produce, in this order:
1. Title slide text: Status Report #[N], [date], consultants with initials, PM.
2. For each workstream, "Workstream #N - Name (consultants)" with two columns:
   Completed ([date range]) and Planned ([next date range]). Each bullet is one
   line, starts with the consultant's initials in brackets, e.g. [AB], and uses
   past tense in Completed and future tense in Planned.
3. Upcoming Schedule table: Event Title | Event Date(s) | Notes.
4. A short checklist: gaps, dates to confirm, anything that looks confidential.

Tone: professional and client-ready. This project [is / is not] under an NDA.
If it is, stop and tell me to compile the report by hand.

Notes:
[paste consultant updates here]
```

## Getting access and help

You need an active Slice membership, and the app must be open to you: either it is public, or the people who run it have added you. If you cannot see **Status reports** on the launcher, ask the people who run it. Ask the VP of Operations about report content, deadlines, the Slides template, or whether a project is sensitive.
