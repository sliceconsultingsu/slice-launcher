# Alumni directory

The alumni directory helps Slice consultants find alumni whose experience fits their career interests, and gives each consultant a few suggested alumni to approach every month. The app is still a work in progress and will change.

## What you can do

1. **Set up your profile.** The first time you open **Alumni directory** from the App Launcher, say what you study, when you graduate, the industries you are interested in and the help you want. It takes about two minutes. You can change your answers at any time under **My profile**.
2. **See your matches.** **Matches** shows three alumni chosen for you, each with the reasons in plain words, such as "Works in Consulting, an industry you chose". You get a new set every 30 days and are never given the same person twice. Earlier matches stay on the page.
3. **Say what you did.** Use **I contacted them** or **Not for me** on a match. Only you and Slice admins see this. It tells Slice whether matches are useful.
4. **Explore alumni.** Search by name or employer, filter by industry, school, major, city, graduation year or the help someone offers, and open a profile to see their jobs, education and the contact details shared with members.

You contact an alum yourself, outside the app. The app does not send messages.

## Use it from your AI assistant

If you have connected an AI app to Slice (see **Connect your AI** on the launcher), it can do the same things under the same rules. Each tool's name starts with `alumni__`.

| Tool                | What it does                                                    | Example request                                   |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `search`            | Finds listed alumni with the same search and filters            | "Find alumni who work in consulting in New York." |
| `get`               | Shows one alum's profile                                        | "Show me this alum's full profile."               |
| `lists`             | Lists the choices for industries, majors, skills and the rest   | "Which industries can I filter by?"               |
| `my_profile`        | Shows your own answers                                          | "What does my alumni profile say?"                |
| `update_my_profile` | Changes your own answers                                        | "Add technology to my industries of interest."    |
| `my_matches`        | Shows your matches and reasons, and gives a new set when due    | "Who are my alumni matches this month?"           |
| `refresh_due`       | For the person running the refresh: lists alumni due a check    | "Which alumni are due for a check?"               |
| `refresh_file`      | For the person running the refresh: files what was read         | "File the result for the alum I just read."       |
| `refresh_graduates` | For the person running the refresh: turns graduates into alumni | "Add this year's graduates to the alumni list."   |

What your assistant reads leaves Slice for that AI app and its provider, so ask it only for what you need.

## For admins

- **Review changes** lists updates the alumni refresh has suggested. Nothing changes a profile until you choose **Approve**. **Reject** leaves the profile as it is.
- **Add alumni** loads rows pasted from the alumni contact book. Loading the same rows twice adds nobody twice.
- **Confirm details** emails each listed alum once, asking them to confirm their job and say which contact details they are happy to share. Read the email on the screen first; sending cannot be undone. Their reply arrives in your own email. Open their profile, choose **Record a reply from them**, and enter what they said. If they ask not to be listed, tick that box and they are hidden.
- On a profile you can hide one contact detail from members, or hide the alum from listings altogether, and undo either.

## Getting access

You need an active Slice membership with **Open Alumni directory** and **Browse alumni, keep a student profile and receive matches**. Admin screens need **Manage alumni data and review proposed changes**. Running the refresh needs **Run the alumni refresh and file proposed changes**. Ask a Slice admin to grant them from the Members page of your account.

## Data and privacy

- Your answers and matches are visible only to you and Slice admins.
- An alum hidden by an admin, and any contact detail an admin has hidden, is never shown to members or to an assistant.
- Contact details come from Slice's alumni contact book, or from the alum's own reply to a confirmation email. Use them to make a considerate first approach, and do not pass them on.
