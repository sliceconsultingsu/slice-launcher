# Alumni directory

The alumni directory helps consultants find former Slice members whose experience matches a student's goals. This version is a learning starter: every person in it is invented, and nothing you do here changes any records.

## What you can do today

1. Open **Alumni directory** from the App Launcher.
2. Browse the sample alumni and student profiles.
3. Read each profile's industry, role and interests to practise spotting a good connection.

## What is coming

The team is building search and filters, real alumni storage, suggested student–alumni matches, AI access through the Slice MCP connection and scheduled updates. Each piece ships through a reviewed pull request; this guide will be updated as it lands.

## Getting access

You need an active Slice membership with **Open Alumni directory** and **Read sample alumni and student profiles**. Ask a Slice admin to grant them from the Members page of your account.

## Data and privacy

The starter contains only fictional people. Do not add real alumni or student details to it, or paste them into an AI tool, until the team has agreed which fields are approved and where they are stored.
